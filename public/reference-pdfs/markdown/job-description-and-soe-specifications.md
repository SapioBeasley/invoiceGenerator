# JOB DESCRIPTION AND SPECIFICATIONS DURING SOE

**Job Title:** JOB COACH  
**Date:** 8/20/20

Under direction of the Program Administrator, this position has responsibility for the support, supervision and training of adults with intellectual disabilities. These clients will vary in capability and support needs and each Job Coach’s assigned clients may change at any time on a temporary or permanent basis. The type of services (in-person or remote) will vary according to client preference.

## Responsibilities include:

- Performing and submitting a daily employee health screening survey.
- Maintaining communication with client homes and caregivers.
- Observe, interpret and report on clients to monitor their progress through behavioral and skill objectives.
- Implementation and documentation of behavioral interventions.
- Implementation of each client’s person-centered Individual Service Plan objectives and care plan.
- Participation in client assessment, planning and evaluation process.
- Collection of client performance data, the writing of progress notes and the writing of special incident reports.
- Awareness of and response to the client’s safety, basic care (including eating and toileting) and medical needs.
- Regular, on-time and predictable attendance is an essential part of this position.

## In-person Services

- Supervising and training clients in the routine acts of community living and work.
- Planning, creating and implementing, with client input, an active and diverse daily schedule of activities.
- Support clients within 10 miles of the last pick-up and/or first drop-off.
- Developing and maintaining a volunteer or paid jobsite (preferably indoor) is strongly encouraged.
- Compliance to the HDS Vehicle Safety policy, including Client Enter/Exit protocol.
- Use, maintenance and insurance of your vehicle in compliance with HDS policies; providing receipts for regular maintenance on your vehicle based on each office’s requirement from Regional Center. This includes pre- and post-shift cleaning of all frequently touched surfaces.
- Management of program expense money provided semi-monthly according to individual client’s Service Plans, according to expenses occurring during performance of the daily schedule and other approved expenses.
- Performance of essential job functions including sitting approximately 3 hours a day, being on your feet approximately 3 hours a day; walking 1–2 hours a day; lifting 15–20 lbs, writing 30–45 minutes a day, and driving 1.5–3 hours a day.
- Reporting to COVID-19 testing twice a month.
- Fully vaccinated against COVID-19.

# JOB SUMMARY

- Starting at 7:30 AM until your last client interaction (clock in and out using the phone).
- Use, maintenance and insurance of your vehicle in compliance with HDS policies; providing receipts for regular maintenance on your vehicle where required by Regional Center.
- Complete staff training sessions once a month for a minimum of 12 hours of approved training each year.

## Job Context

- Outdoors, casual dress, community interactions, some environmental hazards.
- Work hours are 8:00 AM to 3:00 PM Monday through Friday. Lunch breaks and rest breaks will be considered “On Duty” and paid when supervision of assigned clients cannot be interrupted.
- Performance of essential job functions including sitting approximately 3 hours a day, being on your feet approximately 3 hours a day; walking 1–2 hours a day; lifting 15–20 lbs, writing 30–45 minutes a day, and driving 1.5–3 hours a day.
- Regular, on-time and predictable attendance is an essential part of this position.

## MINIMUM EMPLOYMENT REQUIREMENTS

### Education

High school diploma or equivalent is required; some college classes in social services (such as Direct Service Professional courses) are preferred.

### Experience

Must have one-year experience serving people with intellectual disabilities. One-year experience in a program with focus on applied behavioral supports in a controlled, supervised setting (such as a Level 4 facility or behavioral day program) preferred.

### Competency

Have the ability to read, write and converse fluently in English; and maintain First Aid/CPR and CPI certification. Must be able to demonstrate and/or perform CPR/First Aid on the ground.

### Tools

Applicants must have a valid CA driver’s license, a good driving record (no DUI or reckless driving) and a reliable, well maintained insured vehicle that passes the HDS vehicle safety checklist. Minimum insurance set by California on the vehicle used to transport clients is required at all times.

### Skills

Good communication skills; ability to work independently; good organizational skills; sensitivity to the well being of others; ability to give and take instruction effectively and produce results as a member of a service providing team; willingness to share appropriate personal interests and abilities to enhance the experiences and opportunities of our clients are preferred.
