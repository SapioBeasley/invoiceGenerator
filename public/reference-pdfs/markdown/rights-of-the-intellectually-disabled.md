# rights of the intellectually disabled

## RIGHTS OF THE INTELLECTUALLY DISABLED

1. A right to day program and home services that help you learn new and familiar things
and make your life interesting. You should be given services with the right amount of
support to match your needs.
2. A right to being treated with respect, to have your needs kept private from the public
and to not be treated roughly.
3. A right to participate in programs and services that are paid for by the state, no matter
what your support needs are.
4. A right to prompt medical care and treatment.
5. A right to religious freedom and practice.
6. A right to be with others and to go into the community to do things that you need and
would enjoy.
7. A right to physical exercise and recreational activities.
8. A right to be free from being hurt, held down, tied up, locked in a room, given too much
medication, or left without someone to help you when you need it.
9. A right to be free from dangerous activities/treatment.
If you feel you are being denied any of the above rights, please tell someone you trust to help
you. You may also call your Service Coordinator at ELARC at (626) 299-4700.
